A solid iron pole consists of a cylinder of height 220 cm and base diameter 24 cm, surmounted by another cylinder of height 60 cm and radius 8 cm. Find the mass of the pole, given 1 cm³ = 8 g. (Use π = 3.14)

GIVEN

Cyl 1: h₁ = 220 cm, r₁ = 12 cm · Cyl 2: h₂ = 60 cm, r₂ = 8 cm · 1 cm³ = 8 g

✓ Verified
PLAN

Find both cylinder volumes (πr²h), add them together, then multiply by 8 g.

✓ Plan Accepted
STEP 1

V₁ = π r₁² h₁ = 3.14 × 12² × 220 = 99475.2 cm³

✓ SymPy OK
STEP 2

V₂ = π r₂² h₂ = 3.14 × 8² × 60 = 12057.6 cm³

✓ SymPy OK
STEP 3

Total V = V₁ + V₂ = 99475.2 + 12057.6 = 111532.8 cm³
