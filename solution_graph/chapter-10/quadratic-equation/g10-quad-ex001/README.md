# Solution Graph: g10-quad-ex001

**Problem:** Solve the quadratic equation x² − 5x + 6 = 0.

This solution graph captures all 6 valid solution methods for solving this quadratic equation:
1. Factoring (Splitting the Middle Term)
2. Quadratic Formula
3. Completing the Square
4. Graphical Method
5. Sum and Product of Roots (Vieta's Formulas)
6. Trial and Factor Theorem

The equation factors as (x - 2)(x - 3) = 0, giving solutions x = 2 and x = 3.

## Methods Included
- **Method 1**: Factoring by splitting the middle term
- **Method 2**: Quadratic formula
- **Method 3**: Completing the square
- **Method 4**: Graphical (parabola x-intercepts)
- **Method 5**: Sum and product of roots
- **Method 6**: Trial and factor theorem

Each method is broken down into logical steps that the tutor can validate as the student works through the problem.
